             Runspindle: Testing Maintained Workﬂows
                         Under Explicit Authority

                                                Runspindle

                          Research and design proposal | November 2026


                                                 Abstract

         Can better task speciﬁcation, session continuity and independent veriﬁcation make an autho-
     rized workﬂow more useful without broadening the actions an agent may take? Runspindle provides
     a proposed composition boundary for studying that question. This paper deﬁnes accepted work,
     distinguishes incomplete execution from correct refusal, and describes how to measure human inter-
     vention alongside cost and quality. It proposes comparisons against native products, expert-assisted
     use and conventional integration software. A separate model-matched experiment would investigate
     individual harness mechanisms. The study design connects bounded execution, human takeover,
     uncertain-eﬀect reconciliation and separately governed candidate evaluation. It oﬀers hypothe-
     ses and disconﬁrmation criteria rather than results. No new benchmark, deployment, market-size
     estimate, measured savings or return on investment is reported.

Evidence status. This is an original conceptual adaptation. The integrated Runspindle system re-
mains unimplemented. Existing bounded component and synthetic integration evidence is not product
acceptance. Artifact formats, source/output relationships and performed validation are identiﬁed by
the accompanying manifest and receipts; the existence of a PDF alone proves no source build. Scope
and exclusions are recorded in SRC-002-disposition.json. Document validation is separate from
implementation and live acceptance.


1    Research question and unit of analysis

An assistant’s useful output can be a draft, advice, a completed change in another system, or a
correctly bounded stop. These are diﬀerent products. A workﬂow that promises an external result
must establish whether that result occurred; ﬂuent narration cannot supply the evidence. Runspindle’s
research question is whether explicit task contracts and coordinated execution improve usefulness for
a stated workload, relative to alternatives an owner can already use. The answer could be negative
even if the proposed components work.
Deﬁne a task episode by an owner-authorized objective, initial state, permitted eﬀects, selected targets,
time and resource budget, and acceptance test. A task revision changes those bindings deliberately.
A retry is an attempt within a revision, not a fresh grant. An accepted completion is a distinct
terminal outcome that meets both the declared goal and relevant permission conditions. A correctly
refused task is a valuable boundary outcome, but not completed work. A study must not improve its
completion metric by taking actions it should not take.
The object of comparison also matters. A consumer product includes models, instructions, routing,
connectors, interfaces, budgets and safety controls. A model accessed through an API is not necessarily

Runspindle is intended to compose scoped access, a selected managed session, explicit human excep-

limits, stop rule and authority for repeated eﬀects. This paper proposes tests; it does not assert that
the lifecycle has been deployed.

2    Diagnosing stops without weakening boundaries

A visible refusal is an observation, not an explanation. Missing context, absent tools, stale sessions,
missing approval, prohibited eﬀects and planning mistakes can all leave work incomplete. Several
causes may coexist. Classify observable preconditions and actions rather than guessing at hidden
model capability.
Record the requested end state, last observed action, unresolved precondition, applicable boundary,
evidence and remaining authorized options. Separate facts from a diagnostic hypothesis. Missing
write scope supports an authority diagnosis; wrong record selection despite adequate access supports
a perception or planning diagnosis. A lost submission response establishes uncertainty, not failure.
Later success does not prove that an earlier trial had the same inputs, tools or scope.
XSTest and OR-Bench provide precedents for studying exaggerated safety behavior and benign over-
refusal [6, 7]. Their datasets and model conditions do not estimate current commercially recoverable
workﬂow stops. A Runspindle study needs its own population and labels. Prohibited requests and
cases requiring approval belong in boundary testing, not the target for increased compliance.
A permitted repair stays inside existing authorization: retrieving allowed context, using an approved
tool, restoring accurate state or requesting a required decision. Missing authority cannot be converted

3    Human intervention as an observable cost

People contribute work that an agent’s apparent success rate can omit: correcting a goal, ﬁnding
context, transferring data, restoring state, checking results and repairing mistakes. We use tactical
intervention for this hands-on steering. The source’s informal “nudge tax” describes hidden burden,
not a measured tax or an assertion that all human involvement is waste. Governance, identity and
professional judgment remain necessary in many tasks.
Measure tactical work, required governance and unattended delay separately. Deﬁne categories before
collection and avoid counting overlapping time twice. An approval that reveals a defect may involve
both a decision and subsequent repair; distinguish them. Elapsed latency is not labor. Parallel activity
also complicates converting minutes to cost. State the counting rule and uncertainty instead of treating
every wait as paid eﬀort.
The actor can be a customer, vendor operator or evaluator. Moving correction into a vendor queue does
not remove it. Report where work occurs and who bears its cost. Setup, veriﬁcation, exception review
and maintenance also belong in a full comparison. Section 6 uses the frozen canonical accounting
classiﬁcation; eﬀort logs supply observations, not another set of costs to add twice.
The customer-support ﬁeld study in Generative AI at Work measures assisted workers in a particular
setting [17]. Its ﬁndings do not establish equivalent eﬀects for autonomous cross-service systems.
Complementary intangible investment can precede measured beneﬁts [3], but this supplies neither
Runspindle’s acceptance rate nor a promised return. METR’s discussion of developer study redesign

An expert-assisted baseline should disclose coaching budget, tools and manual transfers and count the
expert’s active time. A native product given an artiﬁcially weak request is not an adequate substitute.
Conversely, undocumented preparation or privileges can unfairly favor the expert arm. Evaluate useful
alternatives, not convenient demonstrations.
Intervention records can reveal requirements but are not automatically training data. The proposed
learning path requires separate permission, selection, redaction, attribution and adjudication. A
reaction is neither proof of correctness nor a release decision. Related revisions must remain together
in dataset splits. Credentials and private conversations are not public ﬁxtures; approved aggregates
and synthetic examples should replace unnecessary case detail.


4    A bounded Runspindle execution contract

Seven repositories deﬁne responsibilities, not seven mandatory network services. Core coordinates
durable intent and artifact state. Host binds work to a selected native session. Browser manages
account-isolated UI actions and fenced takeover. Tether routes attributable collaboration events.
Reﬁnery evaluates eligible examples and candidates. Console displays observations and freshness.
Platform owns composition and release gates. These names do not establish interoperability.
The desired lifecycle admits a bounded revision and stable operation identity, retains host, workspace,
account and lease bindings, pauses and fences for exceptions, reconciles ambiguous eﬀects, then vali-
dates, accepts and delivers exact output. Feedback may later enter separately authorized evaluation.
These are explanatory responsibilities; the repository’s versioned contracts remain authoritative.

Durable orchestration preserves intent across interruption; it cannot prove a website submission oc-

transition. A recycled process identiﬁer, stale route or recovered proﬁle is not suﬃcient evidence of
the intended session, account or permission.
Resolve credentials only through approved account- and origin-bound helpers. Trace identiﬁers are not
authorization tokens. Scope cannot migrate silently to another host or account when the selected one
is unavailable. Telemetry should distinguish emitted, queued, rejected and independently observed
events; a local HTTP response does not establish backend readback. Existing masking and native
integration gaps remain explicit, with no complete security or redaction guarantee.
Prior research motivates studying the mechanisms without proving this design. ReAct examines
interleaved reasoning and action [4]. SWE-agent examines interfaces for software tasks [5]. Anthropic’s
dated engineering guidance distinguishes predeﬁned workﬂows from dynamic control and describes
long-running coding harnesses [13, 14]. These are observations about their settings, not Runspindle
results or evidence that unbounded retries will produce reliability.
A controller needs limits on steps, time, expense and allowed recovery. It should stop accurately when
a prerequisite cannot be met. Use deterministic code for a stable step when it satisﬁes the same
contract more simply. A model is a candidate for variable interpretation or choice, not a mandatory
implementation for every transition.
Experiments bind dataset, baseline, parameters and output digests. A favorable evaluation justiﬁes
review, not deployment or broader authority. Release requires the applicable approval and rollback
reference. Improvement is a proposed governed process, not autonomous self-modiﬁcation. Existing
bounded local evidence remains distinct from the unimplemented integrated system and unrun live
acceptance.


5    Workload selection and credible substitutes

Incomplete work is not automatically a market. Begin with an owner who can authorize a legitimate
route, a valuable outcome, feasible independent acceptance and enough recurrence to examine upkeep.
Prohibited routes cannot support a completion promise. Irreducible judgment must remain visible.
Rare tasks may be better served by expert help than a custom integration.
Screen observed work rather than enthusiasm for agents: what happened in recent episodes, which
prerequisite was absent, who repaired it, how often did it recur, and which existing system could have
handled it? Include ordinary successes and routine exceptions. Interview interest establishes neither
willingness to pay nor feasibility. Segment by dominant friction: context assembly, interruptions,
service-speciﬁc actions or veriﬁcation. Test the smallest suitable intervention before constructing a
general platform.
A document-delivery study could assemble permitted material, produce an exact artifact, obtain
required approval and verify delivery. An account-setup assistant could preserve progress while the
owner handles identity and binding acceptance. A connection-management workﬂow could reduce
repeated context entry through already approved integrations with clear revocation. These examples
grant no credentials, create no accounts and authorize no ongoing access. They are study ideas, not
demonstrated features.
An authorized defensive-operations study might compare alert summarization, operator review and
result readback through already approved tools. Remediation still requires its exact action approval.
This example supplies no authority to change security controls or aﬀect another party’s systems. It
illustrates why preparation, decision, execution and veriﬁcation need separate evidence.

6    Economics of accepted work

SRC-005 is the selected economics section within the SRC-002 paper bundle. This original adaptation
is not a historical rebuild or measured economics. Deﬁne the workload, accounting period, currency,
task boundary and acceptance/permission rules before comparing alternatives. A is the count of
distinct terminal outcomes that meet both the declared goal and authorization rules. Retries and
unveriﬁed outputs are not accepted outcomes; failed, rejected and unauthorized attempts still incur
costs.
Accounting identity: C = M + T + H + V + K + R + F; U = C / A for A > 0. M is model expense,
T tools/infrastructure, H human operation, V veriﬁcation, K maintenance, R recovery/repair, and F
allocated ﬁxed integration, onboarding, exception-design and initial evaluation expense. The seven
disjoint period totals use one currency; U is currency per accepted authorized completion. Assign each
cost once: for example, veriﬁcation labor belongs in V rather than also H, and initial evaluation in F
rather than also V. State allocation periods and avoid hiding setup expense outside the comparison.
With A = 0, U is undeﬁned even if C = 0; report incurred C and zero completions. Unknown inputs
remain unknown, not zero. The executable model stores costs in millionths of a currency unit, so its
exact conversion is U = C_micro / (1,000,000 * A).
Compare native assistance, expert-guided assistance and a specialist harness on matched task families
with the same permission and acceptance rules. Report cost alongside acceptance, quality, safety,
latency and human repair; a cheaper attempt need not produce cheaper accepted work. More model
use might reduce repair, but only observation can establish that tradeoﬀ. Reference [1] motivates
evaluating cost together with accuracy; it supplies no Runspindle return-on-investment estimate.
Fixed-cost sensitivity: for A > 0, the identity can be regrouped as U = F / A + (C - F) / A.
This is accounting, not a volume forecast: recurring costs and acceptance can change with workload.
Rare tasks can favor manual help because integration and evaluation must be funded before useful
output. Report the assumed workload mix, period, labor rates, acceptance rate and maintenance
cadence. Include all failures and cleanup on both sides. The source’s schematic amortization diagram
is excluded; its idea is represented here without curves or an estimated crossing. A specialist may
remain more expensive at every volume. No measured break-even point is known.
Synthetic illustration only: the original executable example assumes ten attempts and total cost of 20
USD, including 10 USD ﬁxed integration. Holding every cost ﬁxed while varying A through 0, 1, 5 and

10 gives undeﬁned, 20, 4 and 2 USD per accepted authorized completion. This isolates denominator
sensitivity and neither predicts acceptance nor holds in general when volume changes. More generally,
at ﬁxed positive A an added cost dC raises U by dC / A; at ﬁxed C, lowering A between positive
values raises U when C > 0. Stress maintenance, repair, setup allocation and completion assumptions
together before a real comparison. No measured input, savings or proﬁtability follows from these
invented totals.
Customer value and vendor revenue are diﬀerent quantities. Measure active eﬀort, elapsed time,
rework, audit usefulness and access to otherwise impractical tasks separately; released time does not
automatically reduce cash spending. Subscription, integration and completion fees allocate diﬀerent
risks. An attempt fee can reward extra activity, whereas an accepted-outcome fee can encourage
easy-task selection or disagreement about veriﬁcation. Specify the acceptance test, eligible workload,
exceptions and dispute process in the commercial agreement. This is a comparison of incentives, not
a Runspindle oﬀer, price schedule or forecast.
Provider change can remove a specialist’s advantage through stronger models, built-in workﬂow fea-
tures, improved platform APIs or incumbent products. In one engineering report, Anthropic describes
removing context-reset machinery after moving from Sonnet 4.5 to Opus 4.5 [2]. That is a vendor’s
observation in a particular application-development setting, not a general prediction. The source’s
opportunity diagram is excluded; retain three qualitative scenarios instead: domain-speciﬁc value
persists, the product becomes a narrower complement, or the gap closes and value erodes. Access
restrictions could also eliminate a niche. These are possibilities without probabilities, calibrated axes
or estimated trajectories.
Potential enduring assets include a permissioned task corpus, domain acceptance tests, reliable inte-
gration, skilled exception handling and customer reach. None ensures a lasting advantage. Evaluate
against the current native product, then rerun the same held-out task families after material provider
or platform changes, documenting versions and any task changes. If a previous gain disappears, re-
assess the service rather than carrying the old result forward. Additional generic prompting alone
does not establish durable value.
Complementary investment and delayed beneﬁts: the productivity J-curve literature [3] explains how
intangible investment can precede gains visible in ordinary productivity measures. Track integration
eﬀort and outcomes over time instead of declaring success after one completed task. This is neither a
promise of eventual gains nor a reason to omit setup costs. Runspindle has no measured prices, costs,
refusal rates, savings, break-even point or market size. Historical claims are not Runspindle evidence.

8    Hypotheses and disconﬁrmation

Four proposed claims can fail. Each needs a workload, comparator, budget and threshold chosen
before collection. A broad claim cannot be rescued by selecting only favorable cases afterward.
H1: A bounded intervention recovers some permitted stops. Evidence against this includes
no gain on held-out families, gains explained by changed tools or authority, or apparent success that
disappears under independent veriﬁcation. Correct prohibitions are never the recovery target.
H2: Productized execution reduces tactical human work without hiding or shifting it.
Compare against experts, retain required approval, and include vendor operations and later repair.
Fewer visible interactions are insuﬃcient if hidden eﬀort or errors rise. An active-time result is not
automatically an elapsed-time or cash-savings result.
H3: Speciﬁc mechanisms contribute beyond extra attempts. Model-matched, budget-aware
studies can test context, interface, continuity and veriﬁcation. Uncontrolled diﬀerences limit causal
interpretation. If deterministic software achieves the same outcome more simply, narrow the harness
claim. Interface and workﬂow studies motivate the question without answering it for Runspindle [5, 13].
H4: An advantage survives full cost and material product change. Repeat task families
after provider or platform updates and record version diﬀerences. The economics review describes one
vendor removing a scaﬀold after a model change [2]; this motivates durability testing, not universal
obsolescence predictions. Access loss, upkeep or native-product convergence can invalidate a business
case even when its initial experiment was valid.
Begin with permissioned discovery, baseline pilots and measurement validation. Test the smallest
intervention supported by stop evidence. Reserve holdouts before tuning and plan sample size from
pilot variability. Only then compare broader uses of an engineering budget: conventional integration,
prompt-only assistance or domain-speciﬁc model work. Equal expenditure does not ensure equal
maturity; report development time and constraints. Rejecting the specialist hypothesis in one domain
is a legitimate result, not a universal conclusion about all domains.
A commercial study additionally needs payer identity, support obligations, deployment limits and
an acceptable escalation process. State an operating envelope of workload, delay, required decisions,
support burden and withdrawal conditions. A repeatable task need not support a scalable business.
Lower average error does not establish that remaining errors are acceptable for every consequence
level.
Governance is part of validity: consented sources, task-scoped access and minimum necessary evidence.

9    Counterarguments and conditional conclusion

Providers may absorb common features and have substantial distribution advantages [13, 16]. Do-
main knowledge and customer-speciﬁc veriﬁcation could justify a specialist, but not by default. The
comparison must stay current after native-product upgrades.
Conventional software is a serious substitute. Known procedures and stable interfaces may need no
model. A useful composition can reserve model decisions for variation and use deterministic steps
elsewhere. If an agent adds cost and uncertainty without improving accepted output, removing it is a
successful engineering ﬁnding.
Necessary human judgment is not a defect to erase. Identity, consent and professional decisions can
deﬁne the task’s value. Distinguish those acts from repeated tactical steering. Hiding people in an
exception queue does not prove unattended completion. Accurate handoﬀ and noncompletion can be
useful without being counted as accepted work.
The empirical case remains open. Benchmarks, benign-refusal datasets, ﬁeld studies and vendor
reports have particular settings [6, 7, 8, 10, 17]. None automatically estimates Runspindle adoption,
ROI or commercial opportunity. Population and tool changes can matter as much as model changes.
Failure to reproduce an advantage should narrow the conclusion, not change the denominator.
Runspindle oﬀers a research program: bind an authorized workﬂow, retain identity through inter-
ruptions, distinguish uncertainty from failure, verify results independently and account for all work
required. Compare against useful native products, skilled users and conventional software. Study
recurrence only with explicit authority. Evaluation informs decisions; it does not authorize action.
The proposal succeeds only if those tests show an advantage inside a stated scope and cost. It may
instead identify tasks better served by conventional code, expert help, a native product or a correct
stop. Negative ﬁndings are part of the intended outcome. Compiling or reviewing this document
cannot pass the separate implementation and live-acceptance gates. This is a research proposal rather
than a record of deployed behavior.

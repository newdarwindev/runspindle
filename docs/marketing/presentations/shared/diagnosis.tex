\begin{frame}[label=diagnosis]{Reasons work stops}
\begin{tabular}{p{.27\linewidth}p{.64\linewidth}}
\textbf{Possible cause} & \textbf{Permitted response} \\
A valid restriction & End the action or use an allowed human handoff \\
Insufficient ability & Reduce the task or improve a tested capability \\
Missing integration & Supply permitted context, tools or persistent state \\
Missing authority & Ask the owner for the specific decision or grant \\
\end{tabular}
\par\medskip
These causes can coexist. An uncompleted request alone neither proves a model
could do the work nor justifies overriding its boundary.

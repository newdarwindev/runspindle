\begin{frame}[label=market]{Candidate tasks and alternatives}
A promising study needs recurring work with a clear acceptance state, permitted
access and enough value to cover its support burden.
\medskip
\begin{itemize}
\item Compare with an ordinary API or deterministic workflow when either can solve the task.
\item Preserve expert judgment where the judgment itself is the work.
\item Treat missing access as a boundary, not as a reason to automate around it.
\item Include exceptions, account changes and maintenance in the business case.
\end{itemize}
\vfill
{\footnotesize Task-selection hypotheses only. No market size, adoption, pricing,
customer, career or return-on-investment claim. A technically successful task can
still cost more than its alternative.}
\end{frame}

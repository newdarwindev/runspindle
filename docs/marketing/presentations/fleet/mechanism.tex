\begin{frame}[label=fleet-identity]{Native session and component ownership}
\begin{tabular}{p{.22\linewidth}p{.69\linewidth}}
\textbf{Owner} & \textbf{Required binding or responsibility} \\
Core & Task, revision, operation, policy and durable approval state \\
Host & Explicit host/workspace pin, native session and current lease epoch \\
Browser & Same browser instance, account/origin scope and fenced controller \\
Tether & Authorized request/delivery route and destination-specific receipt \\
Console / Platform & Fresh observations / pinned packaging and qualification \\
Refinery & Reviewed examples, candidate digest and separate release decision \\
\end{tabular}
\medskip
A recycled process ID or a reachable machine does not establish native session
identity. A requested host pin must not silently move to another host.
\vfill
{\footnotesize Proposed boundaries, not evidence of enrolled hosts or live control.
Trace correlation aids diagnosis; it carries no execution authority.}
\end{frame}
\begin{frame}[label=fleet-observation]{Observation and event continuity}
\begin{tabular}{p{.25\linewidth}p{.66\linewidth}}
\textbf{Observation} & \textbf{What can be concluded} \\
Snapshot & A source reported a state at a particular time \\
Event mirror & Ordered records need source identity, cursor and replay/gap handling \\
Freshness & Age and missing updates must remain visible to the viewer \\
External receipt & A separately checked effect can resolve a previously unknown result \\
\end{tabular}
\medskip
A snapshot cannot establish continuous event coverage. Missing or stale telemetry
leaves state unknown; it does not authorize restart, retry or takeover.
\vfill
{\footnotesize Actual source transport, native wire qualification and full system
acceptance remain blocked in the pinned status. No live fleet observation is
asserted by these diagrams or repository files.}
\end{frame}
\begin{frame}[label=fleet-control]{Passive viewing and control transfer}
\begin{tabular}{p{.25\linewidth}p{.66\linewidth}}
\textbf{Boundary} & \textbf{Required control check} \\
Passive human view & Authorized viewing grants no keyboard or pointer control \\
Interactive takeover & Bind a new fenced lease to the same browser and account \\
Handoff / expiry & Reject stale epochs and revoke obsolete input paths \\
Lost action response & Reconcile the external effect before a possible retry \\
\end{tabular}
\medskip
The planned PinchTab action path and KasmVNC view must share the browser identity.
OpenBao references remain inside scoped helpers, outside logs and evaluation data.
\vfill
{\footnotesize Browser runtime enforcement, isolation and native handoff acceptance
are not run. Temporal durability alone does not prove browser fencing or an
external outcome. No platform limit is modified by this proposal.}
\end{frame}
